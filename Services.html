INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY / 01

Trademark

A trademark helps distinguish the goods or services of one business from those of others. It can include names, words, logos, symbols, slogans and other distinctive identifiers.

PURPOSE Distinguishes a brand, product or service from competitors.
EXAMPLES Brand names, logos, symbols and slogans.
PROTECTION Registration is generally for 10 years and can be renewed.
INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY / 02

Copyright

Copyright protects original creative and intellectual works. It gives the creator or rights holder legal protection against unauthorised use, reproduction or certain forms of exploitation of the protected work.

PURPOSE Protects original creative and intellectual works.
EXAMPLES Books, music, artwork, photography, films and software.
PROTECTION The duration depends on the type of work and applicable law.
INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY / 03

Patent

A patent provides legal protection for a qualifying invention. It can give the patent holder exclusive rights to prevent others from commercially using the patented invention without permission, subject to applicable law.

PURPOSE Protects qualifying inventions and technological innovations.
EXAMPLES New technologies, machines, processes and certain innovative products.
PROTECTION Patent protection is for a limited period under applicable law.
